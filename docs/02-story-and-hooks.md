# 02 · Story, hooks and retention

<!-- Copyright 2026 Matheus Patricio Pessoa Santana — framezero (MIT) -->

Nice animation won't save a weak story. These rules decide whether people watch.

## The skeleton that works

```
HOOK (0–2.5 s)  →  CONTEXT (by 3 s)  →  BEATS (2.5–6 s each)  →  PAYOFF  →  CTA (3–4 s)  →  LOOP
```

| Part | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | Stop the thumb. It must be readable **at frame 0**. | "POV: you found the app that PLANS YOUR DAY" |
| **Context** | Promise what's coming and how long. | "5 things it does" · "the last one is the best" |
| **Beats** | One idea each. Show the product doing it. | "2/5 Daily challenge", where a tap lands on the right answer and +25 XP appears |
| **Payoff** | Deliver the open loop. | "and the BEST is #5" |
| **CTA** | One action that fits your launch stage. | "100% free · coming soon · follow to know when it launches" |
| **Loop** | The last 0.4 s wipe into frame 0 so it rewatches. | |

## Frame 0 rules

- **All hook text is visible at t = 0.** Pulses and bounces are fine, but no fade-in, no typewriter, and no black or logo intro.
- **The product is already in frame 0** (the phone with the app), so the first frame works as a thumbnail.
- **Three sizes of text:** a small setup line ("POV:" / "you found an app that"), a HUGE key phrase ("PLANS"), and a colored second phrase ("YOUR DAY"). One pill for the twist ("and it's free").

## Hook formulas that worked for us

| Formula | Example | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **POV** | "POV: you found the app that studies with you" | Tours, lifestyle |
| **Dare / quiz** | "Bet you can't get all 3 (5 seconds each)" | Getting comments. The last question has no answer on screen, and the CTA is "comment your answer" |
| **Pain + time** | "Stuck on homework?" → "and it's 11:47 pm" (the clock ticks) | One feature that solves one pain |
| **List** | "5 things this free app does (the last one is the best)" | Feature tours |

A hook that only speaks to a narrow group ("ENEM in 30 days") filters out everyone else. Use a narrow hook only on purpose.

## Retention mechanics

- **Pattern interrupt every 2–4 s.** Use a new background color per beat, a camera push-in, a label popping, a counter, a scene wipe or a sound.
- **A progress bar** (Stories-style segments at the top) tells people how much is left, so they don't bail.
- **Open loops:** "the last one is the best", "the last question almost nobody gets".
- **Reading time:** ≈ 0.3 s per word, and a label must stay *settled* (not moving) for ≥ 0.8 s.
- **Text-only works.** Most people scroll muted, so the story must work without sound. Sound is the bonus layer.
- **Length:** 15–25 s for a single idea, 30–40 s for a full tour or a quiz. If a beat feels slow in the preview, cut it. Don't speed it up.

## Honesty rules

- Show only real features and real screens.
- No invented numbers ("10k students!"), ratings or testimonials.
- Your CTA must be true *today*. Before launch that means "coming soon / follow". "Link in bio" only once the link exists.
