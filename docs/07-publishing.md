## One caption for TikTok and Instagram

```
<hook line that echoes the video> <emoji> <one sentence of value>
<question that's easy to answer in a comment> 👇
#tag1 #tag2 #tag3 #tag4 #tag5
```

Examples (from the estuda+ series, translated):

- **Tour:** "POV: I found the app that studies with me 😳 and it's free! which of these 5 would you use most? comment the number 👇 #fyp #foryou #viral #learnontiktok #studytok"
- **Quiz:** "bet you can't get all 3 😏 the last one almost nobody gets… comment your answer 👇 and tag who'll get it wrong 😂"
- **Feature:** "stuck on homework and it's almost midnight? 😩 the AI tutor explains any subject step by step, 24/7 🧠 comment "ME" if you study late 👇"

**Why a question:** comments weigh more than likes in ranking. Make the answer tiny (a number, a word, "ME").

## Hashtags

- **Use 5 at most.** TikTok shows "Maximum 5 hashtags" (since 2025) and Instagram also caps at 5. More can't help.
- Mix 2–3 broad discovery tags with 2–3 niche tags. For education: `#learnontiktok`, `#studytok`, `#studytips`, `#edutok`. Regional broad tags help in non-English markets (e.g. Brazil: `#paravocê`, `#viral`).
- Hashtags are a hint, not a growth hack. Watch time and comments matter far more.

## Posting checklist

- Upload the **1080p** file (made from the 4K master) and pick **frame 0** as the cover. If you followed the rules, it's already the best frame.
- Optional: add a **trending sound** in the app at low volume over your track, or replace the track entirely. Your code-made track is the safe default.
- Turn on auto-captions only if there's voice. Text-only videos don't need them.
- Post the same video to TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Don't upload versions with another platform's watermark.
- Reply to the first comments quickly, ideally with a video reply.
- Make a **series** (one feature per video, quizzes 3–4×/week) rather than one big launch video. Consistency compounds.

## Reading the numbers

- 0–100 views: the hook didn't stop people. Change frame 0 and the first 2 seconds, not the ending.
- Views but few likes: the middle is slow. Add interrupts and shorten the beats.
- Likes but no comments: the CTA question is too hard or missing.
- Compare videos of the same length and posting time. A single post means little; trends over 3–5 posts mean a lot.
